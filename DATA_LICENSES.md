## Scope of rights

- CC0 on the dataset applies to the CSV rows and JSON category metadata
  as published by the dataset owner.
- It does **not** transfer to the videos, thumbnails, channel content,
  or external URLs referenced inside the data.
- Treat the dataset as "public domain for analytical purposes" and do not
  imply redistribution rights for underlying YouTube content.
